The clips in `sounds/` are excerpts of these recordings: trimmed, loudness-matched, in some cases
high-pass filtered and with their shortest spikes limited, and encoded as MP3. "Deep" and "High" wind
chimes are the same recording played slower or faster.

White, pink and brown noise and the binaural beats are generated live in `synth.js`.
